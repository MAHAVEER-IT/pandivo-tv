FireTV Music Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how the FireTV Music Companion application collects, processes, and protects your information during your music listening experience on Amazon Fire TV devices.

We are dedicated to user privacy: our architecture is designed to store personal preferences locally on your Fire TV device and minimize remote data collection.

2. Voice & Microphone Audio Processing

The application includes an AI DJ voice query feature. Our handling of voice data adheres to strict privacy safeguards:

  • On-Demand Only: Audio recording initiates solely when you explicitly interact with the Voice Search or AI DJ microphone interface.
  • No Background Audio Capture: The app does not maintain any passive listening or always-on ambient microphones.
  • Immediate Discard: Raw voice audio streams are processed in real-time for speech-to-text conversion and are immediately discarded. No audio recordings are saved to persistent disk or uploaded to external storage buckets.

3. Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini API)

To deliver conversational AI DJ recommendations, the transcribed text of your music intent queries is communicated securely with Google's Gemini API:

  • Query Context: Only text prompts related to musical preferences (e.g., genre, mood, tempo) are sent to the AI service.
  • No Identity Selling: We do not associate personal identifying accounts, credit cards, or tracking cookies with AI query requests.
  • Direct Song Matching: AI responses return structured recommendation parameters that are matched against our catalog algorithms.

4. Local SQLite Data Storage

The application uses an on-device embedded SQLite database (firetv_music.db). The following data is kept strictly on your Fire TV hardware:

  • Liked Tracks & Playlists: Records of songs and playlists you have favorited.
  • Playback History: Your recently played tracks for resuming sessions.
  • Avatar Companion Selection: Your chosen companion avatar (Bamboo, Ember, Frost, Sunny, or Shadow).

None of this personal preference data is transmitted to or synchronized with remote cloud servers.

5. Music Streaming & External Content

Music streaming is delivered via decentralized public content nodes (Audius Protocol) and local storage devices:

  • Audius Network: Audio streaming requests retrieve publicly available music tracks directly from decentralized host nodes. Standard network connection metadata (such as IP address) is handled according to standard Internet protocols.
  • USB Drives: When a USB flash drive is attached, the app scans solely for audio media files (.mp3, .wav, .flac) to populate the local player. No file data is copied or uploaded.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

Because all user state is retained on-device, you maintain total control over your information:

  • You can reset your avatar companion at any time from the Settings screen.
  • To delete all history, favorites, and database records, simply select Clear Data in Fire TV Settings → Applications → Manage Installed Applications → FireTV Music, or uninstall the app.

7. Contact & Updates

We may periodically update this policy as new features are introduced. Any revisions will be reflected on this page.

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data security, please refer to the project documentation repository on GitHub.
