Privacy Transparency & Compliance

Pandivo TV Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Build Version: 1.0.0-RC

Zero Passive Listening
Voice queries are processed on-demand using an on-device C++ acoustic engine (Whistle). No ambient background listening occurs.
Amazon Bedrock AI Engine
Music intents and live DJ narration are curated via Amazon Bedrock (Nova Lite) & Gemini. Transcripts contain zero personal PII.

1. Introduction & Commitment

This Privacy Policy outlines how Pandivo TV (package: com.firetv.musicpoc) collects, processes, and protects your information during your music listening experience on Amazon Fire TV and Android TV devices.

Pandivo TV is engineered from the ground up on privacy-first principles: our living room application has zero third-party advertisements, zero behavioral cross-site trackers, and zero dependency on Google Play Services.

2. Voice Recognition & On-Device Audio Processing

Pandivo TV features an on-device voice search and AI DJ interface. Our handling of microphone input follows strict safeguards:

  • On-Demand Recording Only: The microphone is opened strictly when you press and hold the Voice button on the TV remote or focus on the on-screen mic button.
  • On-Device Acoustic Processing: Audio transcription is executed locally on your TV hardware via our embedded C++ JNI Whistle engine (whistle.cact). Voice recognition does not require streaming raw microphone audio to remote servers.
  • Zero Ambient Listening: The application does not contain background audio listeners, wake-word daemons, or passive room monitoring.
  • Immediate Memory Discard: Raw PCM audio samples are processed in transient RAM and instantly purged upon transcription completion. No audio recordings are written to permanent storage.

3. Generative Artificial Intelligence (Amazon Bedrock & Google Gemini)

To power natural language music search, continuous AI DJ track choreography, and fullscreen song insights, Pandivo TV uses serverless generative AI:

  • Primary Cloud AI: Music intents, continuous DJ transition voiceovers, and song summaries are generated using Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Nova Lite) via AWS Lambda.
  • High-Availability Failover: If AWS routes experience latency or rate limits, requests automatically fail over to Google Gemini Flash or our on-device deterministic rule engine.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): Only music-related queries (such as genres, tempos, mood words, and song titles) are sent for AI inference. No account names, emails, IP addresses, or device serials are shared with AI model providers.
  • No Model Training: Your music queries are not stored, retained, or utilized to train third-party foundation models.

4. Data Storage: Local SQLite & Amazon DynamoDB Cloud Sync

Pandivo TV uses a high-performance hybrid storage model:

  • Local SQLite WAL Mode (Primary): Your selected Companion Avatar (Bamboo, Ember, Frost, Sunny, or Shadow), recently played tracks, liked songs, and custom playlists are stored locally in firetv_music.db with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) for sub-millisecond 60 FPS television responsiveness.
  • Amazon DynamoDB Multi-Device Backup: To allow your preferences to persist if you reinstall the app or use multiple Fire TV devices in your home (e.g. Living Room and Bedroom), your anonymous preferences (avatar ID and liked track IDs) are backed up to an encrypted Amazon DynamoDB single-table (pandivo-tv-cloud-sync).
  • Anonymous Identifier: Cloud sync records are keyed solely by an anonymous TV installation UUID (userId). No email, credit card, phone number, or personal identity is ever associated with your cloud record.

5. Music Streaming & External Media Access

Pandivo TV delivers high-fidelity audio playback through two transparent methods:

  • Decentralized Audius Network: Online streaming retrieves public music files directly from decentralized Audius network host nodes via HTTPS. Connection metadata (such as IP addresses) is processed according to standard RFC Internet protocols by decentralized gateway discovery nodes.
  • USB OTG Drive Media: When a USB flash drive or external SSD is connected to your Fire TV, the app requests storage access solely to scan for local audio files (.mp3, .wav, .flac, .m4a) and extract album artwork. No files are copied, modified, or transmitted outside your local television hardware.

6. User Rights, Data Control & Instant Deletion

You have complete ownership over your data at all times:

  • In-App Avatar Switcher: You can re-select or modify your music companion avatar at any time in the Settings screen.
  • Complete Local Purge: You can wipe all local databases, history, and preferences by clearing data in Fire TV: Settings → Applications → Manage Installed Applications → Pandivo TV → Clear Data.
  • Cloud Sync Purge: Uninstalling the application or selecting "Reset Preferences" removes your cloud backup record from Amazon DynamoDB.
